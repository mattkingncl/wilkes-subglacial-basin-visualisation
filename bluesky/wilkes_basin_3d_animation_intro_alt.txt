Silent 44-second 3D animation. It opens high above the South Pole with the whole Antarctic ice sheet, shaded white to blue by surface height, then swings down to the coast of George V Land, East Antarctica. The ice sheet is drawn as fine vertical columns with 100x vertical exaggeration; the ocean is a flat blue sheet. Purple-to-yellow streaks trace ice flow speed (20 to 500 metres per year) converging into the Mertz, Ninnis and Cook glaciers as the camera flies past each one. The view pulls back and the ice sheet lifts off its bed and vanishes, revealing the bedrock: pale blue where it lies below sea level, green and brown where it rises above it, with the Transantarctic Mountains as tall pale columns on the left. The camera drops to the basin floor and a label appears: Wilkes Subglacial Basin.
