Silent 19-second 3D animation of the ice sheet over the Wilkes Subglacial Basin, East Antarctica, drawn as vertical columns with 100x vertical exaggeration. It starts at the present day, with purple-to-yellow streaks tracing ice flow speed (20 to 500 metres per year) towards the coast. The image dissolves into a computer-modelled ice sheet under a past warm climate with higher CO2, similar to the Pliocene: the ice front has retreated far inland and the deep basin floor lies open to the ocean. After a pause the view dissolves back to the present-day ice sheet and the flow lines return.
