Silent 27-second 3D animation of the East Antarctic Ice Sheet over the Wilkes Subglacial Basin, drawn as fine vertical columns with 100x vertical exaggeration. Purple-to-yellow streaks trace ice flow speed (20 to 500 metres per year) into the Ninnis and Cook glaciers. The camera flies to the ice fronts and back, then the ice sheet lifts straight up off its bed and fades away, leaving the flow lines frozen in place. Beneath is the bedrock: pale blue where it lies hundreds of metres below sea level, green and brown where it rises above it. The camera drops to the basin floor and a label appears: Wilkes Subglacial Basin.
