TITLE
Beneath the Wilkes Subglacial Basin — Lifting the East Antarctic Ice Sheet off its bed

DESCRIPTION
The East Antarctic Ice Sheet over the Wilkes Subglacial Basin is lifted off its bed to reveal the deep marine basin beneath it (BedMachine Antarctica v3).

Animation: Matt King, University of Tasmania.
Vertical exaggeration 100x. Topography drawn as columns; column roughness shows data density.

Data and models:
- Bed and ice geometry: BedMachine Antarctica v3 (Morlighem et al., 2020)
- Ice velocity: MEaSUREs InSAR-based Antarctic ice velocity (Mouginot, Rignot & Scheuchl)
- Coastline and grounding line: MODIS Mosaic of Antarctica (MOA)
- Radar flight lines: Bedmap; bathymetry: IBCSO v2
- Pliocene-like ice sheet: PISM simulation

Data archive: BedMachine Antarctica, the MEaSUREs ice velocity map and MOA2004 were obtained from the National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC), Boulder, Colorado, USA, including the NASA NSIDC Distributed Active Archive Center (DAAC) for the MEaSUREs products: https://nsidc.org
